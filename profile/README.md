## Clipstack Studio

**Someone technical on your side.**

A technical partner for non-technical founders and small companies. We act as your CTO, in plain language: we decide what to build and what to skip, then help build it, and everything stays yours.

- **Fractional CTO:** roadmap, architecture, hiring and vendor decisions.
- **Product development:** web and mobile products, built to last. Native iOS and Android is our signature specialty.
- **AI enablement:** AI tools, agents and automations that your team keeps using after the workshop.
- **Prototype to production:** apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or v0, made secure, stable and ready to launch.
